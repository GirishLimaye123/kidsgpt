2 · AI Talk: AI Can Look, But It Still Guesses

Big idea: AI can look at images and describe patterns, text, objects, colors, and clues. But it does not truly know what happened in the real world, and it can be wrong when an image is blurry, cropped, confusing, or missing context.

✏️

3 · Design Corner: Recommend For A Human

Today's design skill: recommendation design. Kid phrase: do not just sort books; help the right reader find the right next book.

Pick a reader type. Your bookshelf should feel like it was made for someone, not for everyone.

📷

4 · Field Mission: Safe Book Photos

Take photos of 2 to 4 book covers. Covers are enough. You do not need inside pages.

Best photo: one cover at a time, flat, readable title, no glare. Also okay: a small stack of covers if all titles are readable. Do not include: faces, names, library cards, barcodes close-up, addresses, or school papers. Inside pages: skip them today unless the teacher says one tiny excerpt is okay.
🪄
