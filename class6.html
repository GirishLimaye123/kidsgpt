2 · AI Talk: AI Can Look, But It Still Guesses

Big idea: AI can look at images and describe patterns, text, objects, colors, and clues. But it does not truly know what happened in the real world, and it can be wrong when an image is blurry, cropped, confusing, or missing context.

✏️

3 · Design Corner: Facets And Reasons

Today's design skill: faceted search plus recommendation design.

Kid phrase: let people choose by clues, then explain why your app picked something. Real apps use facets like mood, genre, age group, reading level, price, size, or location.
📷

4 · Field Mission: Safe Cover Photos

Take or choose 1 to 3 book cover photos. Covers are enough. You do not need inside pages.

Best photo: one cover at a time, flat, readable title, no glare. Also okay: a small stack of covers if all titles are readable. Do not include: faces, names, library cards, barcodes close-up, addresses, or school papers. Inside pages: skip them today. We are practicing cover vision.
🔨
